About This System

School Social Hub is a prototype internal communication platform designed to improve staff collaboration, classroom visibility, announcements, and cover communication within a school environment.

Technology Used

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, GitHub Pages. Future backend: Google Sheets and Apps Script.

Problem Solved

Reduces fragmented communication across email and informal messages by creating one shared digital space for staff updates and operational notices.
